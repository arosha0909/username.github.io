Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Pennyroo mobile application (the "App") collects, uses and shares information when you use it. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not directly collect personal information such as your name, address or phone number.

The App uses third-party services that may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device information (device model, operating system version, language)
  • Advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • App usage and interaction data, crash logs and diagnostics

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and other information to show ads, measure ad performance and, where permitted, personalise the ads you see.

You can learn more about how Google uses data at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and read Google's Privacy Policy.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

3. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain and improve the App
  • To display advertising that keeps the App free
  • To understand how the App is used and fix problems

4. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App will ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from within the App or your device settings.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the third-party service providers listed above, as needed to operate the App, or when required by law.

6. Data retention and security

Information collected by third-party services is retained according to their own policies. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

10. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@example.com.
